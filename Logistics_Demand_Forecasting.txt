SCENARIO
A logistics data science team at a freight brokerage has been tasked with building a demand forecasting
system for long-distance truckload transportation. Reliable price forecasts are critical: carriers and shippers
must negotiate contract rates months in advance, and inaccurate forecasts lead to margin erosion or lost
capacity.
The team has access to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Producer Price Index (PPI) for General Freight
Trucking, Long-Distance Truckload (NAICS 484121). This monthly index tracks price changes from the
perspective of the seller — the trucking company — and serves as a key leading indicator of freight market
conditions.
This is where you come in. You are hired as a data science consultant. Fresh from successfully completing
Data Science course, you now have the skills and tools to analyse this time series, uncover seasonal
patterns and trends, and build forecasting models that can predict future PPI values for the truckload freight
market.

DATASET DESCRIPTION
The dataset contains a monthly time series of the BLS Producer Price Index for the long-distance full truckload
general freight trucking industry (BLS series ID: PCU484121484121), covering the period December 2003 to
August 2018. The index is anchored to a base of 100 in December 2003. Note that the dataset provided may
contain data quality issues that must be identified and resolved before modelling.

ATTRIBUTE DESCRIPTION
observation_date The first calendar day of each month representing the observation period. Acts
as the time index for the series. Format: YYYY-MM-DD.
PCU484121484121 The PPI index value for long-distance full truckload general freight trucking
(NAICS 484121). Base period December 2003 = 100. Higher values indicate
rising producer prices in the trucking sector.
