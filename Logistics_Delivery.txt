The delivery time prediction model plays a crucial role in the logistics industry, where accuracy in delivery is of highest importance. 
Delivery time directly impacts customer satisfaction and influences their overall experience.

The features of this dataset are as follows. They are self-explaining in the context of order delivery. 
The target feature is "Delivery_Time". Your main task with this dataset is to train Machine Learning model(s) in order to predict 
the delivery time of an order.
