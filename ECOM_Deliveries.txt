The delivery success prediction model plays a crucial role in the e-commerce logistics industry, where accuracy in delivery success is of highest importance. 
Delivering on time directly impacts customer satisfaction and influences their overall experience.

The features of this dataset are as follows. They are explained below and also many of them are self-explaining in the context of logistics delivery. 
The target feature is "Delivery_OnTime". Your main task with this dataset is to train Machine Learning model(s) in order to predict 
if the delivery will be on time or not.


 #   Column               
---  ------                
 0   ID                    ID Number of Customers.
 1   Warehouse_block       Big Warehouse of the company is divided in to block such as A,B,C,D,E.
 2   Shipment_Mode         The Company Ships the products in multiple way such as Ship, Flight and Road.
 3   Customer_Calls        The number of calls made from enquiry for enquiry of the shipment.
 4   Customer_Rating       The company has rated from every customer. 1 is the lowest (Worst), 5 is the highest (Best)
 5   Product_Cost          Cost of the Product in US Dollars.
 6   Historical purchases  The Number of Prior Purchase.
 7   Product_Priority      The company has categorized the product in the various parameter such as low, medium, high.
 8   Gender                Male and Female. 
 9   Discount_rate         Discount offered on that specific product.
 10  Weight(gr)            
 11  Delivery_OnTime       Target
